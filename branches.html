1906 – 1910

George A. Castor, Congressman

  • Born Aug 6, 1856 in Holmesburg (the 1910 book; some modern sources give 1855, to be checked). Elected to the 58th Congress Feb 19, 1904; re-elected; died Feb 19, 1906 at “Stonyhurst,” his Holmesburg home. Married Katharine Duffy.
  • Descent: Hans Georg → George (1736) → Mathias (1766) → William (1797) → George Mathias (1830) → George A.
  • Siblings: Mary (1858, m. Lemuel Ball), Nellie Mills (1864, m. Dr. Richard Allen Martin, then W. H. Allardyce; mother of the 1910 compiler George Castor Martin), Morton (1866–1869).

1809 – 1910

The Wissinoming line of George Johnson Castor (1802–1870)

  • Farmed at Wissinoming (Tulip & Howell Streets); school director 1850–62; trustee of the Frankford Church. Married Elizabeth Bender (1809–1900). Nine children, incl. Henry B. (1832, last Castor to farm the homestead), Howard (1835), Linford (1837), Barton Jenks (1839, conveyancer, firm Castor & Hood, Frankford Arsenal).
  • Howard (1835–1909): farmed Castor Road, then near Rockland, Delaware (1874); builder in Wissinoming from 1876. His son Howard Paul (1859) built much of Wissinoming and promoted Torresdale Avenue; sons Norman S. (1875) and William B. (1874) founded Castor Brothers coffee & tea, Frankford and Camden.

1744 – 1855

Frederick Castor (1739–1800) and his sons

  • Lieutenant in the Pennsylvania Militia, wounded at Brandywine. Married Elizabeth Rorer, 1764. Nine children, incl. George (1765, “Cork-Leg George,” owner of the Castor Mansion on the Delaware), Daniel (1776, died in New Orleans), twins David and Samuel (1777).
  • General Samuel Castor (1777–1855): Pennsylvania Militia; escorted Lafayette from Trenton to Philadelphia in 1825. Married Hannah Dyre. Children: Hiram (1809, Lt. Colonel), Dyre (1813–1845, private secretary to Henry Clay, Washington Monument fund), Margaret Myers (1815–1888).
  • George’s son Jesse Yonker (1794–1846), City Solicitor 1825 & 1828, father of Lt. Thomas F. Castor (1822–1855, West Point, First Dragoons, died Fort Tejon, California).

1760 – 1884

Jacob Castor (b. 1742) and the Frankford carriage-builders

  • Married Sophia Rorer (1760), then Catharine Fox. Second Lieutenant, Bucks County Militia, 1777. Farm on Oxford Road. Eleven children incl. Jacob, Henry (landowner on Frankford Avenue, died 1814), John (1773), George Fox, Isaac (proprietor of the “Jolly Post” hotel).
  • John (1773) and Catharine Knorr bought the homestead at Oxford and River Roads in 1803; sons Peter (1805) and Thomas (1810–1884). Thomas is documented as a Philadelphia carriage and railway-car builder (rolling-stock history site). The 1833 founding date of Castor Carriage Works is from your notes, unverified.

1736 – 1811

George Castor (1736–1811) and Mathias (1766–1835)

  • George: farmer on Salter Lane, Oxford Twp.; British took his horses in 1778. Mathias bought the 79-acre “Pennypack Farm” on the Delaware in 1801; married Rebecca Johnson, 1792. Children: Margaret (m. Josiah Jackson), Samuel, William (1797), Phebe, George Johnson (1802), Rebecca (m. Joseph Biles).
Source: G. C. Martin, The Castor Family of Pennsylvania (1910), cross-checked where noted. A revised 1986 edition by Charles G. Castor (“A Castor family in America”) is catalogued at FamilySearch and HSP and is the next source to consult.
