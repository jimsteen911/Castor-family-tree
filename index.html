Research integrity

Sources & method

The tree is a research project, not a claim that every connection has been proven. Source quality and uncertainty remain visible.

01

Primary published source

The Castor Family of Pennsylvania, G. C. Martin, 1910. The principal published source for the early Philadelphia family.

Read the 1910 book at Internet Archive ↗
02

Family records

Recent generations and several South Jersey details come from family notes. These entries are identified as family records and should be independently corroborated.

Family record · verification ongoing
03

Unproven connections

The relationship between Edward Castor of Pittsburgh and Hans Georg Gerster has not been established. The site deliberately leaves that link open.

Open research question
04

Research standard

Each person and relationship should carry a source citation, confidence level and research note. Do not infer parentage from proximity, surname or geography alone.

Respecting family privacy

The previous site suppressed full birth dates for living people. This redesign continues that practice and displays year-only dates for living individuals where available.
